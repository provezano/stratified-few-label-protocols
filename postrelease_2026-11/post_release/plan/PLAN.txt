# P1 fresh-table confirmation: frozen plan

Status: FROZEN at the time recorded in `PLAN.sha256`, before any foundation model (FM) was run on any table of the
frame below. This is a frozen analysis plan with a SHA-256 hash, not a pre-registration: no public registry holds it.
The plan, the frame (`frame.csv`), the per-table predictions (`predictions.csv`), the frozen model coefficients
(`frozen_model.json`), the inclusion rule of the harvest and the code are hashed together (`PLAN.sha256`).

## 0. Why, and what has been seen

All six blind reviewers of P1 said that the newer-source non-replication is "hard to address; only new data would
answer it". This study collects new data. It tests, on tables that no earlier P1 analysis has touched, every claim of
the abstract and of the contributions list.

Seen before this plan (disclosed):
- Everything about the 20 development (DEV), 52 PMLB/UCI, 20 newer-source tables and the coverage-test (Study 17)
  tables, including the diagnosis of the newer-source non-replication (the diagnosis specification,
  SHA-256 7a263e29…, its addenda and an internal note). The sealed tables enter only as the aggregate
  numbers in the paper.
- The pilot of this plan (`results/pilot_sf.csv`, `results/pilot_pmlb.csv`, `results/power.csv`): the quantities of
  every hypothesis below, computed on the stored newer-source and PMLB/UCI fits with the code used for the analysis.
  It is the source of the power analysis and of the expectations stated with each hypothesis.
- Fresh tables (this frame): metadata, column names, n, d, C and the class counts after encoding. No model was fitted,
  and no feature-label statistic was computed. The table traits that enter the predictions are functions of n and the
  class counts only.
- Code check: `code/run_unit.py` re-ran one stored newer-source unit (z22133098_trees, TabICLv2, arm T, 180 fits);
  seeds, attempts, coverage, macro-F1, accuracy and balanced accuracy are bit-identical to the stored Study 15 file.
- `code/analyze.py` was run once on the stored newer-source fits as a pipeline test (tmp folder). It reproduces the
  diagnosis remainder of the paper's model (−0.013) and gives the pilot values quoted below.

## 2. Design (identical to the P1 frames)
- Study 15's arm T, reused unchanged through `simaudit` `runner/study13.py` (`code/run_unit.py`): row permutation
  (namespace 35), cell seeds (namespace 34), redraw seeds, base seed 20261020, table IDs = source offset + record
  number (Zenodo 10¹⁰, figshare 2·10¹⁰, Dataverse 4·10¹⁰, Mendeley 5·10¹⁰; non-numeric records via CRC-32), so every
  seed is new.
- Strategies: stratified (5 reps), random (20 reps), redraw (random draws redrawn until they cover every class,
  20 reps; reused when the first draw covers). m ∈ {1, 3, 5, 10} labels per class, budget B = mC, scored on the
  complement of the labelled rows. Arm F (fixed split) is not run: no hypothesis uses it, and arm-T seeds do not
  depend on it.
- FMs: TabPFN v2 (Real-TabPFN checkpoint, tabpfn 9.2.0), TabICLv2, Kumo-Tabular, TabPFN v3.5, through the same
  wrappers and pins as the earlier frames. CPU, one thread per job.
- **Device rule.** The development, PMLB/UCI and newer-source frames ran on Apple-silicon CPUs (host A; the
  newer-source manipulation also on host B). This study runs only on Apple-silicon CPUs (host A, host B; host C only if
  needed): one device class. `simaudit` code on host B is byte-identical to the local copy (SHA-256 of `study13.py`,
  `fm.py`, `harness.py`, `study2.py`, `study4.py` checked), and host C was earlier shown bit-identical for TabPFN v2,
  TabICL and Kumo.
- **Failures.** A unit (table × FM) is retried up to three times. A table without all four FMs is dropped from every
  analysis (complete case). If fewer than 30 tables remain, the primary family is reported as underpowered, with the
  numbers.

## 3. Per-table quantities (`code/confirm_lib.py`, `code/analyze.py`)
Means over reps within (strategy, m), then over m:
- gap_M = stratified − random, m ∈ {1, 3, 5, 10}, for M = macro-F1, balanced accuracy (BA), accuracy (the P1 unit).
- balance part bal_M = stratified − redraw, m ∈ {3, 5, 10}; coverage part cov_M = redraw − random, m ∈ {1, 3, 5, 10};
  bal1, cov1: the same at m = 1.
- FM-mean = mean over the four FMs.
- Pre-data traits (`code/predict.py`, from n and the class counts): binary, log10 n, r = C·π_min, V = mean over
  m ∈ {3, 5, 10} of the expected squared distance of a random composition from balance, q = mean over m of the exact
  probability that a random draw misses a class.
- Mechanism terms (from the fits, as in the diagnosis): cost of misses pred_cov (Monte Carlo 4,000 draws) and signal
  (BA of random draws at m = 10, rescaled).
- Flips: a comparison of two FMs on one table and budget is clear when both protocols separate them by more than 0.01
  in macro-F1. Protocol flip: the winner differs between stratified (5 reps) and random reps 0–4; noise flip: random
  reps 5–9 against random reps 10–14 (matched 5-rep means).

## 4. The frozen prediction
`frozen_model.json` (written by `code/freeze_model.py` from the stored traits of the 72 standard tables):
- **pre (primary prediction):** FM-mean macro-F1 gap ~ 1 + q + V + binary + log10 n, OLS on the 72 development and
  PMLB/UCI tables. Every term is known before any fit, so the predicted gap of every fresh table is in
  `predictions.csv` now.
- **m2 (secondary):** the paper's model (DIAGNOSIS_SPEC, without the frame indicator), refitted on the same 72 tables:
  gap ~ 1 + pred_cov + V + binary + signal + log10 n. Its coefficients are frozen now; its inputs pred_cov and signal
  come from the fits.
- **pre92 (descriptive):** the pre-data model refitted on all 92 seen tables.
Pilot (20 newer-source tables, seen): the pre-data model ranks them (Spearman 0.58) but over-predicts their mean gap by
0.032 (slope 2.1); the paper's model ranks them at 0.78 with a remainder of −0.013. If the fresh tables resemble the
newer-source tables, H-pred is expected to pass on ranking and to show a mean over-prediction; the result is reported
either way.

## 5. Hypotheses, tests and decision rules
Tests are two-sided unless stated; a test passes when its Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 within its family **and** its
estimate has the predicted sign. Decision intervals: table bootstrap, 5,000 resamples, seed 20261108, percentile 95%.

**A small gap is not a failure.** The macro-F1 claim is tested as agreement between the predicted and the observed
per-table gap (H-pred), not by the size of the gap. A small observed gap confirms the claim when the frozen model
predicted it small. The per-FM S − R macro-F1 tests (D-gap) are descriptive and are read against the prediction.

### 5.1 PRIMARY family (Holm over 2)
| ID | Claim (abstract / contributions) | Test |
|---|---|---|
| H-pred | "Table traits predict where the gain appears" (abstract); "table traits predict where stratified labelling overstates macro-F1 … as a model fitted on the standard tables predicts" (contribution 3) | Spearman(pre-data prediction, observed FM-mean gap) > 0, one-sided |
| H-cov1 | "With one label per class, the whole gain comes from random draws that miss a class" (abstract; Proposition) | TOST on the FM-mean balance part at m = 1 (stratified − redraw), margin ±0.02 (the P1 coverage-test margin) |

Decision rules and wording:
- **H-pred confirmed** if the Spearman test passes **and** the calibration holds: the 95% interval of the slope of
  observed on predicted includes 1, or the 95% interval of the mean residual (observed − predicted) includes 0.
  Wording: "on fresh tables published after every FM, traits fitted on the standard tables predicted the per-table
  gap (Spearman ρ, mean residual)".
- **Weaker version** if the Spearman test passes and the calibration fails: "traits rank where the gain appears on fresh
  tables, but the standard-table model mis-sizes it by [mean residual]". The abstract keeps "predict where", not
  "predict how much".
- **Not confirmed** if the Spearman test fails: the abstract sentence is limited to the frames where it held, and the
  failure is diagnosed (Section 7) before anything is written.
- **H-cov1 confirmed** if TOST passes: "holds on all four frames". Not confirmed: the theorem stands (it is a proof),
  and the paper reports that the fresh-table estimate could not establish equivalence within ±0.02, with the interval.
- Pilot power (resampled newer-source tables, Holm over 2): H-pred 0.87 at 25 tables, 0.93 at 30, 0.97 at 35; H-cov1
  0.67 at 25, 0.74 at 30, 0.87 at 35. **The frame needs about 35 tables**; the harvest stops at 40.

### 5.2 SECONDARY family (Holm over 13)
| ID | Claim | Test |
|---|---|---|
| H-bal.fm1, H-bal.fm5 | "With more labels, unequal class counts add to it, most for the two TabPFN models" | balance part (S − redraw, m ∈ {3,5,10}) > 0 for TabPFN v2 and TabPFN v3.5, Wilcoxon |
| H-bal.order | same ("most for the two TabPFN models") | mean balance part of the two TabPFN models minus that of TabICLv2 and Kumo-Tabular > 0, Wilcoxon |
| H-BA.fm (4) | the protocol inflates balanced accuracy (RQ2, contribution 1 "predicts which metrics move") | S − R BA > 0 per FM, Wilcoxon |
| H-acc.fm (4) | "Stratified labelling also lowers accuracy on imbalanced tables" | S − R accuracy < 0 per FM on the imbalanced tables (r = C·π_min ≤ 0.6, i.e. binary minority share ≤ 0.30), Wilcoxon; not evaluable if fewer than 8 such tables |
| H-miss.cost | "miss-share report" (abstract, contribution 4): the miss share, with its cost, predicts the coverage part | Spearman(cost of misses pred_cov, observed FM-mean coverage part) > 0, one-sided |
| H-pred.M2 | the paper's model ranks fresh tables | Spearman(m2 prediction, observed FM-mean gap) > 0, one-sided |
| H-rank | "changes which model wins close comparisons" | per-table protocol flip rate − noise flip rate > 0, Wilcoxon |

H-rank enters the secondary family because the pilot power is ≥ 0.8 at ≤ 35 tables (1.0 at 20).

Decision rules and wording:
- **H-bal:** confirmed if all three pass: "on all frames tested, balance adds to the gap beyond one label per class,
  most for the two TabPFN models". Weaker: H-bal.order passes but one TabPFN model is not separately positive:
  "balance adds most for the TabPFN models" with the per-FM numbers. Not confirmed: the abstract sentence is restricted
  to the development and coverage-test frames. (Pilot power at 35 tables: fm1 0.64, fm5 0.89, order 1.0; the fm1 test
  is underpowered and a failure of it alone leads to the weaker version.)
- **H-BA:** confirmed per FM; "for all four FMs" only if 4/4 pass.
- **H-acc:** confirmed per FM; "lowers accuracy on imbalanced tables" kept for the FMs that pass.
- **H-miss:** two parts. (a) Reproducibility (decision interval, not in the Holm family): the share of stored random
  draws that miss a class equals the exact probability from the class counts, i.e. the 95% interval of the mean
  difference lies within ±0.03. Then the paper can say that the miss share can be computed from the class counts
  before any labelling. (b) H-miss.cost passes: the miss share weighted by its cost predicts the coverage part. The miss
  share alone is reported descriptively; in the pilot it does not predict the coverage part (Spearman −0.53), which is
  what the paper already says ("the miss share alone does not predict the gap").
- **H-pred.M2:** a pass means the paper's mechanism model transfers; its mean remainder on fresh tables is reported
  next to the newer-source remainder (−0.013).
- **H-rank:** confirmed if it passes. The fresh flip rate is reported next to the PMLB/UCI rate recomputed with the same
  four FMs and definition (pilot: 0.37 against noise 0.12; newer-source 0.59 against 0.13).

## 6. Paper consequences (fixed now)
The non-replication subsection reports the fresh-table result with numbers whatever the outcome, and the paper gains
a claim-by-frame table: for each claim of the abstract and contributions, which frames support it (development, PMLB/
UCI, sealed, newer-source, coverage-test, fresh), using the verdict labels above. The abstract's wording follows the
decision rules in Section 5. No re-run, re-seed, re-encoding or table change follows any outcome.

## 7. Failure diagnosis (fixed now)
Any primary or secondary claim that is not confirmed is diagnosed before any text is written, with the method of
an internal note: set medians of miss share, cost of misses, signal, imbalance and binary share against
the standard and newer-source sets; the transfer residual of the paper's model; and the per-FM split. The diagnosis
is exploratory and is labelled so.
