# Disclosed deviations from the frozen plan (PLAN.sha256)

## Deviation 1 (2026-11-08): queue scripts skip a unit that failed three times instead of stopping the study

**What happened.** The study started 2026-11-07 20:09 (-03). The local queue stopped at 23:06 after unit
`d_EF0JED_birdpresence fm1` (TabPFN v2) failed three times; host B, gated until 03:42, stopped at 08:23 on 2026-11-08 after
`d_EF0JED_birdpresence fm5` (TabPFN v3.5) failed three times. 139 of 160 units had finished (73 local, 66 host B, no
overlap); 21 were missing, of which these two had failed.

**Cause.** Both failures are the same deterministic error in TabPFN's preprocessing
(`TabPFNValidationError: All features are constant and would have been removed`). The table has d = 3 discrete
features (3, 16 and 10 distinct values). In at least one labelled set the labelled rows are identical on all three
features, so TabPFN refuses to fit. The seeds are fixed, so the three attempts reproduce the same labelled set and the
same error: the failure is a property of the table under the frozen design, not of the hosts. TabICLv2 and
Kumo-Tabular fitted the same labelled sets without error.

**Plan rule that applies.** PLAN.txt section 2: "A unit (table x FM) is retried up to three times. A table without all
four FMs is dropped from every analysis (complete case)." `code/analyze.py` already implements the complete-case drop
(`missing_complete_case`). The table `d_EF0JED_birdpresence` is therefore dropped; 39 tables remain (plan target about 35).

**Infrastructure fault.** `code/queue.sh` (frozen SHA-256 42df5e3b...) did not follow that rule: on the third failure of
one unit it wrote `ALL_FAILED` + `ALL_DONE` and released the whole host, and `code/pull.sh` (frozen a2fe3e26...) then
wrote `queue/ALL_FAILED` and stopped the study. A secondary cosmetic error: `queue.sh` read `claims_other.txt` before
`pull.sh` had first written it (stderr message, no effect).

**Change.** `queue.sh` now records such a unit in `queue_<host>/FAILED_UNITS.txt`, skips it and continues, and creates
`claims_other.txt` at start. `pull.sh` exchanges the failed-unit lists between hosts (so neither host retries the
other's failed unit), finishes when every unit has a fit or has failed three times, writes the union to
`queue/FAILED_UNITS.txt`, and then runs the frozen `code/analyze.py` once, as before. Under 30 tables the analysis
still runs and the primary family is reported as underpowered (plan section 2), rather than the study stopping.
Frozen originals are kept byte-identical in `plan/deviations/frozen_code/`.

**Not changed.** `run_unit.py`, `analyze.py`, `family.py`, `confirm_lib.py`, the frame, the predictions, the frozen
model, the seeds, the FMs and their pins, the device class (Apple-silicon CPU: host A and host B only), and the
retry count. No unit was re-run with a changed setting. No fit had been analysed when this change was made.

**Effect on the analysis.** None beyond the plan's own complete-case rule: the fresh frame is analysed on 39 tables.

## Deviation 2 (found 2026-11-09, after the analysis; no re-analysis of the planned tests)
The inclusion rule dated Kumo-Tabular's release to 2026-09-23. A desk-editor simulation questioned this, and a check of the Hugging Face API (2026-11-09) found two dates: the model repository was created on 2026-09-01, and the public commit "Release Kumo Tabular" is dated 2026-09-28. The blog post is dated 29 September. Three frame tables (z22960380_citizenstatus, f34004118_hfref and z22997149_menohealth, first published 2026-09-25 to 09-27) therefore appeared before Kumo-Tabular was public, though after all four models had been trained.
- Exploratory sensitivity: `code/sens_kumo_release.py` → `results/sens_kumo_release.json`. On the 36 remaining tables, ρ(M2) is 0.86, ρ(pre) is 0.73 and the mean M2 remainder is −0.002. The FM gaps are essentially unchanged.
- Disclosed in the main text (Sec. 5.1) and in the supplement (app:postrelease).
