# Balance-mechanism probe for P1: frozen plan (2026-11-08)

This plan is frozen: its SHA-256 is recorded in plan/PLAN.sha256 before the first probe fit. It is NOT a
pre-registration, because no public registry is used. The study is exploratory and confirmatory in the sense of a
frozen plan, and is reported as such.

## 1. Question and claim under test
The objection is that the balance part of the stratified-labelling gap beyond one label per class was confirmed only
for the two TabPFN models. The stored fits show that the balance part splits into two parts:
- a scatter part (prior-proportional exact class counts minus covered random counts), positive for all four FMs;
- a prior-shift part (balanced minus prior-proportional counts at fixed coverage), whose sign is FM-specific and follows
  each FM's prior response. Diagnosis: an internal diagnosis note.

Claim tested: "Balance adds to the gap for all four FMs through the scatter of random class counts. The further gain
from a fully balanced context is FM-specific and follows each FM's prior response."

## 2. Tables, and what is new

plan/frame.csv, set = confirm). These tables are NOT untouched. Their confirmation balance parts
(stratified minus covered random, `bal_mf_fm*` in confirm_2026-11-08/results/fresh_pertable.csv) have already been
analysed and reported. H-response therefore reuses these already-reported confirmation fits on its outcome side. Only
the probe fits are new. H-scatter and the secondary hypotheses use the new probe fits only.

Held-aside check, 4 tables (set = heldaside). These are the post-release tables the confirmation harvest accepted on

## 3. Probe design (code/probe.py)
The design is the evidence study's composition manipulation, unchanged:
- fixed 30% test split (namespace 36);
- pool = remaining rows; nested class-wise row orders per rep; one FM seed per (m, rep);
- complement of the pool unused.

Levels at fixed coverage:
- λ = 0 (prior-proportional exact counts), 0.5, 1 (balanced);
- V (a covered random composition: a random draw of m·C pool rows, redrawn until every class is present; its class
  counts are then filled from the nested row orders).

Settings:
- m ∈ {3, 5, 10} labels per class, 5 reps, 4 FMs (TabPFN v2, TabICLv2, Kumo, TabPFN v3.5);
- seed namespace 62 (new; the evidence study used 61); base seed 20261020;
- identical compositions are fitted once.

Metrics on the test split: macro-F1, balanced accuracy and accuracy.

## 4. Quantities (per table x FM; means over reps within m, then over m)
- scatter = MF(λ0) − MF(V)
- shift = MF(λ1) − MF(λ0); shift_BA and shift_ACC likewise
- probe balance part = MF(λ1) − MF(V)
- predicted shift = c0 + a·shift_BA + b·shift_ACC, with c0, a, b FROZEN in plan/frozen_coefs.json:
  - fitted on the stored evidence fits, 40 tables x 4 FMs, R² 0.80;
  - c0 = 0.00109, a = 0.8239, b = 0.2991.
- predicted balance part = scatter + predicted shift
- λ* = argmax over {0, 0.5, 1} of MF
- r = C · π_min of the pool

## 5. Hypotheses and tests

Primary family, Holm over 2, α = 0.05:
- H-scatter: the scatter part is > 0 for every FM.
  - Test: per FM, a one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank over the 43 tables.
  - Intersection-union: p = the largest of the four p values.
- H-response: per-FM balance parts follow the measured prior response.
  - Statistic: over the 39 tables with all four FMs, the within-table-centred Spearman correlation between the
    predicted balance part (probe) and the confirmation balance part (bal_mf_fm*, independent draws, already reported).
  - One-sided permutation p: FM labels of the prediction are permuted within each table, 20,000 permutations, seed 20261108.

Secondary family, Holm over 3:
- H-λ*: the mean λ* of the two TabPFN models exceeds that of TabICLv2 and Kumo. Per-table difference, one-sided Wilcoxon.
- H-shiftsign: the mean shift part of the two TabPFN models exceeds that of TabICLv2 and Kumo. Per-table difference,
  one-sided Wilcoxon.
- H-imb: the mean shift part of TabICLv2 and Kumo increases with r. One-sided Spearman.

Data handling:
- A (table, FM, m, rep) cell counts only if all four compositions were fitted without error. Fit errors are recorded,
  not retried: seeds are fixed, so failures are deterministic.
- A table enters a per-FM test only if it has at least one complete cell for that FM.
- H-response uses only tables with all four FMs.

## 6. Reported descriptively (no test)
- Per FM: mean and 95% bootstrap CI of the scatter, shift and probe balance parts.
- Efficiency (mean shift_BA / |mean shift_ACC|), against the frozen predicted order v3.5 > v2 > Kumo > TabICLv2.
- Mean λ*.
- Within-table calibration slope of the outcome on the prediction, and the pooled Spearman correlation.
- The 4 held-aside tables alone.
- Fit errors, and the host of each unit.

## 7. Decision wording
- If H-scatter passes, the paper states: "Balance adds to the gap for all four FMs through the scatter of class counts
  (fresh tables)."
- If H-response also passes, it adds: "The further gain from a fully balanced context is FM-specific and follows each
  FM's measured prior response (BA gained vs accuracy lost); per-FM balance parts on fresh tables were predicted from
  coefficients frozen on earlier tables."
- In either case the paper states that H-response's outcome reuses already-reported confirmation fits.
- If a primary hypothesis fails, the current wording stays ("balance part beyond one label per class confirmed for the
  two TabPFN models"), and the failure is reported with its numbers.
- Secondary results are reported as supporting or not, never as headline.

## 8. Power (pilot on the stored 40 tables, already seen; results/pilot.json)
- H-scatter: the smallest stored per-FM effect is TabICLv2 +0.013 with 15-16 of 20 tables positive. At n = 43, power is
  above 0.95 for each FM at α = 0.025.
- H-response: on the stored 40 tables (prediction from the manipulation fits; outcome stratified minus random at m ≥ 3,
  separate fits), the within-table Spearman is 0.68 (permutation p 2e-4).
  - Resampled power at n = 39 and α = 0.025 is 1.00 (500 resamples).
  - Using the probe balance part directly instead of the frozen prediction gives 0.87 (descriptive only, not tested).
  - Pooled Spearman: 0.60.
