# Study 4, Stage A (FM5): label selection on DEV. Report of 2026-10-21

Pre-registration: prereg/STUDY4_LABEL_SELECTION.md (Amendment 1). Data: 20 DEV datasets, 4 budgets B = mult·C, 10 strategies (S-RR with 10 reps, the others with 5), 4,000 labeled sets. Generated by the `simaudit.analysis.study4` functions. All numbers are DEV only; HELD-OUT stays sealed.

## Reading
1. **RQ-L1 (protocol).**
   - Stratified sampling, which knows the labels, beats realistic random in macro-F1 at every budget: +0.143 at B = C, +0.085 at 3C, +0.075 at 5C, +0.034 at 10C, all Holm p ≤ 0.02. In accuracy the gap is not significant (+0.032 / −0.008 / +0.006 / −0.018).
   - The gap comes mostly from class coverage. Random covers 70% of the classes at B = C and 94% at 3C, and an unseen class scores F1 = 0.
   - The "m per class" protocol of the pilot and of most of the literature is therefore optimistic in macro-F1.
2. **RQ-L2 (literature baselines; confirmatory contrasts).**
   - k-medoids vs random: +0.056 at 3C (Holm p = 0.019) and +0.028 at 10C (p = 0.026). The other two budgets are not significant.
   - TypiClust vs random: +0.072 at 3C (p = 0.001). The other budgets are not significant, and TypiClust is worse at 10C.
   - In accuracy the gains are small and not significant after Holm: TypiClust +0.032 at 3C (p = 0.066), k-medoids +0.019 at 10C (p = 0.071).
   - k-center, ProbCover and the outlier filter do not beat random.
   - Σ-opt reaches +0.096 at B = C before correction. It fails on datasets with many duplicates: the graph becomes disconnected and it picks one row per component.
3. **Decomposition.** Medoids per class, a diagnostic that uses y, beat stratified random by a further +0.085 at B = C and +0.067 at 3C. The position within the class matters on top of coverage.
4. **RQ-L4 (headroom).** The best of 10 random draws, chosen on one half of the rows and scored on the other (split-half), sits above the random mean by +0.198 / +0.131 / +0.115 / +0.050. In sample the headroom is only slightly larger, so the variation between draws is real, not noise from evaluating on the test rows. This is far above the B01 gate (0.03 at B ≤ 3C).
5. **RQ-L3 (interaction with transduction).** LLGC on the FM5 prior hurts at mult ≥ 3 under every strategy: mean −0.004 to −0.035, with a 10% quantile of about −0.04 to −0.13. Good selection does not make LLGC safe. The exception is medoids per class at B = C (+0.023).
6. **Sensitivity.** Scoring only on rows that no strategy selected in the same repetition leaves the Δ values almost unchanged, within ±0.01.
7. **Gate to Stage B (other FMs).** k-medoids and TypiClust leave the ROPE, so Stage B runs with FM1, FM2 and FM3 (`study4-a --learner fm1|fm2|fm3`).
