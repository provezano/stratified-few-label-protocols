## Reading (hand-written 2026-10-28 after the single analysis run; everything below it is generated)

**Bottom line.** Study 7's main DEV result replicates on T1′, with effects of the same size:
- the stratified protocol is optimistic in macro-F1 for all four FMs;
- under the realistic random protocol, TabICL beats TabPFN v3.5.

The "tie under stratified" half of the ranking claim does not hold. On T1′, TabPFN v3.5 is slightly *ahead* of TabICL under stratified sampling.

### Confirmatory family (6 contrasts, Holm)
1. **(i) stratified − random, macro-F1, mean over 1C–10C: replicated for 4/4 FMs.**
   - Gaps with 95% CI and Holm p:
     - TabPFN v2: +0.069 [0.058, 0.079], Holm 5e-9;
     - TabICL: +0.037 [0.028, 0.047], Holm 2e-7;
     - Kumo: +0.052 [0.043, 0.062], Holm 1e-8;
     - TabPFN v3.5: +0.088 [0.077, 0.099], Holm 3e-9.
   - Win-rate 0.90–0.96; the sign is stable under leave-one-dataset-out.
   - Every DEV estimate lies inside its T1′ CI. T1′/DEV ratios: 0.94–1.17.
   - **C1 replicates.**
2. **(ii-a) TabICL − TabPFN v3.5 under random (3C–10C): replicated.**
   - +0.047 [0.037, 0.057], Holm 2e-8, win-rate 0.88. DEV: +0.046.
3. **(ii-c) TabICL − TabPFN v3.5 under stratified: no tie. The verdict is "difference".**
   - −0.009 [−0.016, −0.004]; TOST p = 0.43 (Holm 0.43); two-sided Wilcoxon p = 0.005.
   - The difference is small, inside the ±0.01 margin as a point estimate, but not certifiably equivalent. Bayes P(ROPE) = 0.76.
   - It comes from the multiclass tables: −0.025 there, against −0.002 on binary tables.
   - **Ranking claim: "half supported"**, the same status as on DEV. The protocol still changes the winner: under stratified, TabPFN v3.5 is marginally ahead; under random, TabICL is clearly ahead.

### Pre-declared secondary analyses
- **S-1, redraw** (random draws conditioned on covering every class; only 30% of draws cover at 1C).
  - Coverage explains almost the whole gap at 1C, and at m = 1 redraw equals stratified as predicted.
  - From 3C on, the gap remains for the prior-sensitive TabPFNs:
    - TabPFN v2: +0.043, Holm 4e-8;
    - TabPFN v3.5: +0.063, Holm 2e-9;
    - Kumo, smaller: +0.016, Holm 6e-4;
    - TabICL: none, +0.003, Holm 0.21.
  - TabICL stays ahead of TabPFN v3.5 under redraw: +0.051 [0.041, 0.061]. The reversal is not explained by coverage.
  - This matches the predictions: TabPFN gap > 0, reversal > 0.
- **S-2, realistic AUC** (an unseen class gets a fixed score, so its AUC is 0.5).
  - The stratified − random AUC gap at 1C is +0.094 to +0.102 for every FM (Holm 1e-8). With the Study 7 seen-class convention it is +0.002 to +0.007, not significant.
  - That convention silently drops the 45% of random 1C fits with one seen class.
  - The prediction is confirmed: AUC hides the protocol bias only because of how missed classes are scored.
- **S-3, ranking flips.**
  - 34.6% [28.6, 41.1] of decisive pairwise comparisons (6 learners, macro-F1) flip winner between the protocols.
  - The noise baseline (random vs random halves) flips 8.5%, a difference of +0.26 [0.20, 0.32]. Accuracy shows the same pattern: 31.5% vs 8.8%.
  - The prediction holds, with a slightly higher rate than on DEV (28.5%).
- **S-4, binary vs multiclass** (35 / 17 tables).
  - The (i) gap is larger on multiclass tables for every FM. The difference is significant only for TabPFN v2 (Mann–Whitney p = 0.03) and, at 1C, TabPFN v3.5 (p = 0.045).
  - The 1C prediction ("larger for multiclass") is therefore only partly supported.
  - The (ii-a) reversal is larger on binary tables: +0.058 vs +0.025, p = 0.002.
- **Accuracy.** Stratified − random is slightly *negative*, −0.016 to −0.026, and mostly not significant. As on DEV, the optimism is a macro-F1 / balanced-accuracy effect, not an accuracy effect.
- **Balance option (post hoc, secondary).** It raises TabPFN v3.5's macro-F1 under random by +0.016 to +0.036 and costs up to 0.05 accuracy. TabICL − TabPFN v3.5 (bal) under random is +0.002 to +0.022, positive at 3C and 5C.
