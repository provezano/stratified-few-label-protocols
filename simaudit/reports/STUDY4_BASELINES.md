## Reading (2026-10-22)
Exploratory. With 12 selectors per Holm family, few cells clear Holm. The means and their consistency across the four FMs carry the reading.

- **USL** is the strongest of the three Amendment 3 baselines.
  - Against random, macro-F1 Δ is +0.066, +0.050, +0.048 and +0.008 at 1C, 3C, 5C and 10C; Holm-significant for all four FMs at 3C and 5C.
  - It ties LUCoS at 3C–5C (Δ +0.000, −0.002) and trails it at 1C (−0.037) and 10C (−0.021). None of these differences is Holm-significant.
  - It sits with the cluster-based group (k-means central, k-medoids, TypiClust).
- **RDSS** gives a small, mostly non-significant gain over random in macro-F1 (+0.018 to +0.038) and none in accuracy. It trails LUCoS by −0.085 at 1C.
  - With γ = 0.5 on z-scored X, the kernel is narrow, so the representativeness term is weak at small budgets.
- **ZCore** is **worse than random** at every budget: −0.079, −0.113, −0.100 and −0.031 macro-F1, and it is the worst selector overall.
  - It has the lowest class coverage of any strategy (0.62 at 1C vs 0.70 for random; 0.85 at 3C vs 0.94).
  - Its top-B score ranking has no diversity step, so it picks clustered high-score rows. ZCore was designed for coreset pruning at large keep-fractions, not for budgets of a few rows per class.
- **Raw k-medoids (S-KMR, Amendment 4)** gains +0.065 over random at 1C, on a par with USL (+0.066) and below Σ-opt, TypiClust and k-means central. From 3C on it is near random (+0.000, +0.012, −0.007).
  - LUCoS beats it from 3C on (Holm-significant at 10C for all four FMs). At 1C the difference is not significant.
  - This matches the LUCoS paper's pattern; see the revised Reading in `STUDY4_LUCOS.md`.
- **Overall.** No new baseline beats LUCoS at any budget. LUCoS remains the selector to beat: at 1C every selector trails it by ≥ 0.004 in mean (Σ-opt is closest at −0.004), and at 3C–5C only k-means central, k-medoids, TypiClust and USL come within 0.025 of it.
